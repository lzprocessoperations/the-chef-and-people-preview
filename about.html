About The Chef & People

Built from hospitality, not from a recruitment desk.

The Chef & People was founded by Dario Zannoni after more than 20 years working inside professional kitchens, including over a decade at Executive Chef level.

The story

Hospitality experience first.

Before recruitment, there were kitchens, services, teams, openings, menus, suppliers and the daily pressure of running hospitality operations.

That experience shaped The Chef & People. We understand what businesses need because we have worked inside the environments they operate in.

We also understand candidates differently. A title on a CV does not always tell you how someone performs when the kitchen is full, the team is under pressure or standards need to be maintained every day.

Our approach

Practical, direct and built around the business.

We focus on understanding the operation first, then finding the right solution.
