Privacy Policy

Information about how The Chef & People may collect and use information submitted through this website.

Important: This privacy policy is currently draft website content and should be reviewed before the website is published.

Information we may collect

We may receive personal information that you choose to provide when you contact us, submit an enquiry, send your CV or communicate with us about recruitment services.

This may include information such as your name, email address, company name, employment history, CV details and the content of your message.

How we may use your information

Information you provide may be used to respond to enquiries, discuss recruitment requirements, communicate about vacancies, introduce candidates and employers, and provide recruitment-related support.

Candidate information

If you provide a CV or information about your professional experience, that information may be used to assess your suitability for relevant hospitality opportunities.

Candidate information should only be shared with prospective employers where appropriate and in connection with recruitment activity.

Employer information

If you contact us about hiring staff, we may use the information you provide to understand the vacancy, your business requirements and the recruitment support you need.

Data retention

Personal information should only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or where retention is required for legitimate business or legal reasons.

Sharing information

Personal information should not be sold to third parties. Information may be shared where necessary to provide recruitment services, comply with legal obligations or support legitimate business operations.

Security

Reasonable steps should be taken to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

Your rights

Depending on applicable data protection law, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion or restriction of your personal information.

Cookies and website analytics

If cookies, analytics tools or third-party tracking services are added to this website in the future, this policy should be updated to explain how they are used.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, services or legal requirements.

Contact

If you have questions about privacy or how your information is handled, please contact The Chef & People using the contact page .
