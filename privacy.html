Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how The Chef & People collects, uses and protects personal information when you contact us, use this website or submit your CV.

1. Who we are

The Chef & People is a trading name of SnippetGB Ltd, company number 13805679.

For privacy-related enquiries, contact:

info@thechefandpeople.co.uk

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the website or contact us, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your telephone number
  • Information included in an enquiry
  • Your CV and employment history
  • Information about the type of role you are looking for
  • Information provided by businesses looking to recruit staff

3. How we use your information

We may use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to enquiries
  • Discuss recruitment requirements
  • Assess suitability for hospitality roles
  • Contact candidates about relevant opportunities
  • Introduce suitable candidates to prospective employers
  • Provide consultancy or other requested services
  • Manage our business relationship with you

4. Candidate information

If you send us your CV or apply for a role, we may use the information you provide to assess whether your experience is suitable for current or future hospitality opportunities.

Where appropriate, relevant information may be shared with a prospective employer in connection with a potential role.

We will not intentionally share your CV with an unrelated organisation for purposes outside recruitment or the services you have contacted us about.

5. Client and business information

If you contact us on behalf of a hospitality business, we may retain relevant contact details and information about your recruitment or consultancy requirements.

This information may be used to provide the requested service and maintain our business relationship with you.

6. Legal basis for processing

Depending on the circumstances, we may process personal information where it is necessary to respond to your request, provide a service, take steps before entering into a contract, comply with legal obligations or pursue legitimate business interests.

7. How long we keep information

We aim to keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

The appropriate retention period may depend on the type of information, our relationship with you and any legal or business requirements.

8. Sharing your information

We may share personal information where necessary to provide recruitment or consultancy services.

For example, candidate information may be shared with a prospective employer where this is relevant to a recruitment opportunity.

We may also use service providers that support the operation of our website or business.

9. Keeping your information secure

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no website, email system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your rights

Depending on applicable data protection law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete information in certain circumstances
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To make a privacy request, contact: info@thechefandpeople.co.uk

11. Cookies and website information

The website may use basic technical functionality required for the site to operate.

If analytics, advertising or other non-essential cookies are introduced in the future, this policy may be updated and additional cookie controls may be required.

12. External links

This website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, website or legal requirements.

14. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact:

info@thechefandpeople.co.uk
